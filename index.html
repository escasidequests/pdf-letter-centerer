PDF Letter Centerer

Puts every page of your PDF at its true physical size, centered on a US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) page. Your file never leaves your computer — everything runs in this browser tab.

When printing, choose “Actual size” (or 100% scale), not “Fit to page”, or your printer will resize it.

Orientation is picked per page: wide pages go on landscape letter, tall pages on portrait.

Free and open source. No uploads, no tracking.
